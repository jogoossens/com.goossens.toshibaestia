Connect to your heat pump

Test the connection, then continue.

Before you continue, you need:
  • A Toshiba BMS-IFMB0UEW-E (or similar) Modbus RTU interface, wired to the heat pump's AB bus.
  • A Modbus TCP gateway connected to the interface and reachable on your LAN — Waveshare RS485-WiFi/ETH, Elfin EW-11, USR-W630, or similar.
Enter the gateway's IP below (not the heat pump's). The gateway translates Modbus TCP from Homey to RTU on the wire.
Gateway

Slave address (DIP S3 bits 1-6). Default 1.
